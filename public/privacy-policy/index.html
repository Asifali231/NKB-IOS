Welcome to Nikah Blessing ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our matrimonial mobile application.

By using Nikah Blessing, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

We use the collected information for:

  • Sending important notifications about your account and matches

3.1 Camera Permission Usage

The camera permission is used for:

  • Capturing profile photos for user verification
  • Face detection and verification features

Camera access is only used when explicitly requested by the user and can be revoked at any time through device settings. Photos captured through the camera are stored securely and used only for profile verification and matchmaking purposes.
