Nikah Blessing

Child Safety Standards Declaration

1. Introduction

Nikah Blessing (the "App") is committed to the safety of all users and to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on our platform. This page declares how we comply with child safety standards and our policies against CSAE and CSAM.

We do not allow users under 18 years of age. Our App is a matrimonial service intended solely for adults seeking marriage. We take our responsibility to protect children seriously and expect all users and staff to do the same.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

Nikah Blessing has zero tolerance for:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — any visual depiction involving a minor in sexually explicit conduct, including photos, videos, or computer-generated imagery.
  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) — including grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or any behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

We prohibit the creation, storage, sharing, or distribution of CSAM and any conduct that constitutes CSAE on our platform. Violations will result in immediate account termination, reporting to relevant authorities, and cooperation with law enforcement as required by law.

3. Age Restriction and Account Integrity

  • Users must be 18 years of age or older to create an account and use Nikah Blessing.
  • We collect and verify date of birth during registration and reserve the right to request identity verification.
  • Accounts found to belong to users under 18 are terminated, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

4. How We Address CSAM and CSAE

When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM or CSAE on our platform, we:

  • Remove the content and suspend or terminate the responsible account(s) without delay.
  • Report to the appropriate authorities and designated bodies as required by applicable law (including, where applicable, NCMEC and similar bodies).
  • Preserve evidence as needed for law enforcement and do not retain CSAM beyond what is necessary for reporting.
  • Act in accordance with our published standards and all applicable child safety laws.

5. In-App Reporting and User Feedback

We provide an in-app mechanism for users to report safety concerns, including possible CSAM or CSAE:

  • Users can report content or behavior from within the App (e.g., report buttons on profiles, messages, or in Help/Support).
  • Users can contact our support team from within the App (e.g., Help Center, Contact Us, or support email accessible from the app).

Reports are reviewed promptly, and we take appropriate action in line with this policy and the law. We do not require users to leave the App to submit a report.

6. Compliance with Child Safety Laws

Nikah Blessing complies with applicable child safety laws in the jurisdictions where we operate, including laws relating to CSAM, CSAE, and the protection of minors. We cooperate with law enforcement and designated authorities when required and support industry efforts to combat online child sexual exploitation and abuse.

We align our practices with recognized guidance, including the Tech Coalition's best practices for Combating Online Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSEA) where relevant to our service.

7. Child Safety Point of Contact

For child safety matters, including CSAM/CSAE prevention and compliance, you may contact our designated Child Safety point of contact:

This contact is available for users, authorities, and trusted partners to reach us regarding child safety. We respond to legitimate inquiries in accordance with our procedures and legal obligations.

8. Content and Conduct Standards

We enforce community and safety standards that prohibit harmful content and behavior, including anything that exploits or endangers minors. Profile photos and content must comply with our guidelines; we reserve the right to remove content and suspend or ban accounts that violate these standards.
